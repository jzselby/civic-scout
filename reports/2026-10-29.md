## Top stories

- **City redevelopment board balks at selling Parry Block for a fifth of its appraised value**: At its Oct. 13 meeting, Salt Lake City's Community Reinvestment Agency (CRA) board took no vote on selling the Parry Block to Ulysses Development for $2.5 million, against an appraisal of about $12 million. Directors asked for an updated appraisal, an equity stake for the city and small-group talks. The board also approved about $9M in commercial loans 7-0 but pulled a loan for 321 Main Street (Chenzo LLC), and delayed changes to its tax increment policy. ([SLC Council actions](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45782))
- **Northpoint rezone stalls amid truck-traffic complaints; residents push back on a possible Emigration Canyon fuel pipeline**: Minutes of the Sept. 22 council meeting show members voted 7-0 to defer the Northpoint rezone from agricultural (AG-2) to light manufacturing (M1-A) after neighbors objected to truck traffic. The draft ordinance adds a data center prohibition. Residents and Rep. Angela Romero urged the council to oppose a possible Sinclair/Phillips 66 fuel pipeline through Emigration Canyon. The council also passed the Wells on 5th townhome rezone by Castlewood Development 7-0 and pulled Tracy Scott Cowdell's appointment as appeals officer with no action. ([SLC Council actions](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45794))
- **Private foundations put $16.5M into Salt Lake City projects as council worries about thin reserves**: Minutes of the Sept. 15 work session show Budget Amendment No. 1 included $11.5 million from the Bezos Earth Fund for Fleet Block and the 9 Line, and $5 million from the Larry H. and Gail Miller Family Foundation for Ballpark NEXT.
  - Members raised concerns about the fund balance, now $12.17M.
  - Straw polls backed $50,000 for a mailer on zoning changes, with no audible vote from Council Member Puy.
  - Straw polls also backed $50,000 in aid for District 2 flood victims.
  
  ([SLC Council actions](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45797))
- **Bond-funded Warm Springs Park project on hold indefinitely; 600/700 North rebuild removed 74 parking spaces**: Minutes of the Sept. 22 work session say the Warm Springs Park project has no dates set until a building footprint is decided and talks with tribal partners are held. The 600/700 North rebuild removed 74 on-street parking spaces and is due to finish Nov. 13. Members backed grants for Poplar Grove flood victims. Crews have replanted 200 trees on North Temple to replace ones lost to poisoning. ([SLC Council actions](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45767))
- **$17.8M freezer warehouse expansion planned on Salt Lake City's west side**: Core X SLC filed for a 66,261 sq ft freezer warehouse addition to an existing building at 3190 W Professional Cir. It is the largest project filed this period. ([SLC permits & planning](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Building&TabName=Building&capID1=26HIS&capID2=00000&capID3=08754&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))
