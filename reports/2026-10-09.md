## Top stories

- **SLC redevelopment agency weighs discounted Parry Block land sale and $9M in loans.** On Oct. 13, the CRA board will consider a discounted land sale to Ulysses Development for a 116-unit housing and arts project on the Parry Block. It will also vote on $9,030,501 in commercial development loan requests from seven applicants. Also on the agenda: a revised lease that would have transitional housing tenant HomeInn Rio Grande (428 W 300 S) start paying rent, and financing for Rio Grande District public improvements. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1114087.html))
- **HF Sinclair to brief SLC Council on pipeline expansion; Ballpark rezoning nears vote.** On Oct. 13, HF Sinclair representatives will brief the council on the proposed expansion of its Westline Connect Pipeline and potential impacts to Salt Lake City. The council also gets a follow-up on rezoning former Ballpark properties from Public Lands to MU-11/MU-5, ahead of a tentative Oct. 20 vote, and a briefing on new e-bike and e-motorcycle rules for city trails. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1114091.html))
- **Taylorsville mobile home park pool shut over extreme chlorine levels.** Inspectors closed the Majestic Meadows indoor pool (4580 S 1055 W) on Oct. 8 after measuring chlorine at 50–75 ppm, far above the 10 ppm limit, and finding no registered pool operator on staff. It is the pool's second suspension within a year, which carries a minimum 7-day closure, and the pool has not reopened. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
- **101,000 sq ft spec warehouse moves into construction in Ninigret Park.** The Ironhorse speculative office/warehouse at 4750 W 2100 S filed plumbing and electrical permits this week, including a $360K electrical package tied to building permit BLD2026-00787. ([SLC permits – plumbing](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Building&TabName=Building&capID1=26BPB&capID2=00000&capID3=01212&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=); [electrical](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Building&TabName=Building&capID1=26BEL&capID2=00000&capID3=01682&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))
