## Top stories

- **Salt Lake City may allow small-lot homes by right in single-family neighborhoods.** The City Council holds a public hearing Nov. 10, continuing Nov. 17, on an ordinance that changes height, setback, lot width, coverage and flag-lot standards in the R-1, R-2, SR-1 and SR-1A zones. It would also create a permitted-by-right "Small Lot Dwellings" use, affecting single-family neighborhoods citywide. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1119205.html))
  *Earlier:* council briefed on small-lot housing changes ([Oct. 16](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1115957.html)) and on housing zoning changes ([Oct. 22](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45662)). New today: the hearing dates and the ordinance details.

- **Ogden plans to divert $3.3 million in property taxes, mostly school district money, to the Wildcat Square area.** The Ogden City Council holds a hearing Nov. 17 on the Wildcat Square Community Reinvestment Area plan. The plan would send $3,330,116 in tax increment over 15 years to a 6.6-acre project area, and about $2.19 million of that would come from Ogden School District. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1118947.html))
