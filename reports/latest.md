## Top stories

- **Weber County to vote on 2027 budget and higher parks fees**: On Tuesday, Oct. 27, Weber County commissioners vote to adopt the tentative 2027 operating and capital budget and to amend Parks and Recreation fees, which affects county taxpayers and parks users. They will also consider a contract with the U.S. Marshals Service to house federal prisoners. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1118005.html))
- **Summit County weighs new zone that would open Brown's Canyon ranch land to Ivory development**: On Oct. 28 the Summit County Council discusses an ordinance creating a Lost Creek Community Zone. The zone would allow development on Garff Rogers Ranch land in Brown's Canyon, represented by Ivory Development. The agenda also includes a closed session on property acquisition, 2027 budget talks and a ridgeline home exception. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1118027.html))
- **HF Sinclair won't rule out running a fuel pipeline through Salt Lake City**: Minutes from the Oct. 13 meeting show every Salt Lake City Council member raised concerns about the Westline Connect pipeline route through the city's watershed and neighborhoods. HF Sinclair said the route would follow the 1950s Chevron corridor, which has leaked before, and declined to commit to excluding the city. Council members were also briefed on Ballpark rezoning changes that add about five acres of open space. ([SLC Council actions](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45701))
- **Salt Palace overhaul gets its first permit, with two exhibit halls coming down**: A $3 million Phase 1 building permit for the county's Salt Palace Convention Center and Entertainment District project at 100 S. West Temple covers demolition of Exhibit Halls 2 and 3 and the north meeting-room stack. The rest of the convention center and Abravanel Hall stay open during the work. ([SLC permits & planning](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Building&TabName=Building&capID1=26HIS&capID2=00000&capID3=08619&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))
- **West Jordan noodle shop closed for serving customers with no water**: Health inspectors closed One Noodle Bar at 1793 W. 7800 S. on Oct. 23 after finding it open and serving customers with no water supply. Inspectors cited 10 critical violations, including raw meat stored over ready-to-eat food. The restaurant has not reopened. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
