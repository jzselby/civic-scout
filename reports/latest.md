## Top stories

- **Salt Palace site could be rezoned for mixed use.** The property owner has filed a zoning amendment application to convert the Salt Palace Convention Center site at 55 S 300 W to a mixed-use development. The application was filed Oct. 15. ([SLC permits & planning](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Planning&TabName=Planning&capID1=26HIS&capID2=00000&capID3=08401&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))

- **SLC Council votes Oct. 20 on jail land-use ban and Ballpark rezone.** The council may adopt an ordinance prohibiting community correctional facilities and jails as a land use, and may rezone former Ballpark parcels to MU-8 and MU-5. The consent agenda sets Nov. 10 and Nov. 17 hearings on the Expanding Housing Options small-lot amendment and a hearing on a $36,000 TSA airport badging fee. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1115959.html))

- **SLC leaders raise concerns about HF Sinclair pipeline.** HF Sinclair briefed the council Oct. 13 on the proposed Westline Connect Pipeline. The council and mayor issued a joint statement citing concerns about drinking water, the watershed and seismic risk. Acting as the CRA board, the council also backed HomeInn Rio Grande transitional housing and delayed a discounted Parry Block land sale for more discussion. ([SLC Council actions](https://www.slc.gov/council/october-13-2026-meeting-recap/))

- **Provo votes on redeveloping Towne Centre mall and Shady Acres mobile home park.** On Oct. 20 the council takes up a general plan change and rezone of about 23 acres requested by Brixton Capital and PEG Development. Related citywide ITOD text changes would allow 77 townhomes; the Planning Commission recommended denying those changes 8-0. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1116121.html))

- **Intermountain downtown hospital and Redwood Road QuikTrip go to SLC Planning Commission.** On Oct. 28 the commission votes on a QuikTrip gas station at a used car dealership at 424 S Redwood Rd. It also holds a work session on Intermountain Health's campus at 45 E 800 S, which includes a 10-story, 221-foot hospital, a 7-story medical office building and a 505-stall garage. A mayor-initiated Planned Development rewrite with tenant protections and housing replacement is also on the agenda. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1116059.html))

- **SLC updates utility impact fees for the first time since 1999.** On Oct. 6 the council approved new water, sewer and stormwater impact fees. The same meeting included $50,000 in emergency aid for Poplar Grove families flooded by a main break. ([SLC Council actions](https://www.slc.gov/council/october-6-2026-meeting-recap/))

## Connections

- **Sugar House assessment collapsed after business opposition.** At an Aug. 18 hearing, business owners and the Utah Restaurant Association opposed the Sugar House assessment, citing construction losses ([minutes](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45515)). The Sugar House Chamber & Community Alliance retracted its request Oct. 14. The city now says it will not proceed with Assessment Area DA-SHBD-27 and will stop tallying protests ([notice](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1115985.html)). The council is briefed and acts on the matter Oct. 20.

- **Intermountain's downtown campus is moving on two tracks.** Aug. 25 council minutes show a hearing was set on an Intermountain Health alley vacation ([minutes](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45546)). The Planning Commission now has the hospital campus plan in its Oct. 28 work session. Those same minutes show the Northwest Community Plan vote was postponed and a hearing was set on a Northpoint industrial rezone that the Planning Commission opposed.

- **The 900-acre airport wetland sale is advancing.** The council was briefed Sept. 8 on selling about 900 acres of airport wetlands to Utah DNR, with a Utah Open Lands conservation easement ([minutes](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45441)). Hearing notices followed on the [parcel disposition](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110603.html) and the [easement's public benefit](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110605.html), and the council took public comment Oct. 6. The Sept. 8 minutes also show unanimous support for mailing residents about Expanding Housing Options.

## Coming up

- **Oct. 19**: The Utah Board of Higher Education trustee summit covers a regional framework for a "reimagined" USHE, a Campus Safety Task Force report, and free speech and institutional neutrality panels. No votes are listed. ([notice](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1115913.html))
- **Oct. 20**: The SLC Council work session covers Local Building Authority bonds for library projects, with $5,985,000 in preparation funding. The projects are an Anderson-Foothill rebuild, a Day-Riverside expansion and Main Library improvements. The session also includes a 75-minute Expanding Housing Options briefing, water conservation and clean energy updates, and a possible closed session. ([notice](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1115957.html))
- **Oct. 22**: The Wasatch Front Regional Council votes on moving a $20M West Davis Corridor interchange at 1525 West in Farmington to Phase 1. It also votes on an extra $2M for 3900 South widening and $10.5M for I-215 work between SR-201 and North Temple. ([notice](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1115921.html))
