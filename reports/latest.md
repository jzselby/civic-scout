## Top stories

- **SLC utility impact fees would roughly triple, the first increase since 1999.** The water impact fee for a 3/4-inch meter would rise from $1,871 to $5,577, and the stormwater fee per quarter acre would go from $374 to $1,993. A new meter-based sewer fee of $4,300 would apply to a 3/4-inch meter. The increases phase in at 70% on Jan. 1, 2027, and reach full amounts July 1, 2028. The council holds a hearing and may vote Tuesday, Oct. 6. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1107993.html))
- **Salt Lake City weighs a citywide ban on new jails and community correctional facilities.** An Oct. 6 hearing covers removing both as conditional uses, which would prohibit them citywide. State- and federally owned property would be exempt. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110613.html))
- **Domo lays off 175 in American Fork.** The Utah tech company filed a WARN notice for 175 workers, with layoffs dated Sept. 23. ([WARN layoff notices](https://jobs.utah.gov/employer/business/warnnotices.html))
- **Murray proposes closing Liberty Elementary.** The closure would start in 2027-28 and would redraw boundaries for four elementary schools (Grant, Longview, McMillan and Parkside) and two middle schools (Hillcrest and Riverview). The public hearing was Oct. 1, and a final vote is set for Dec. 10. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1108981.html))
- **Ellerbeck Mansion owners seek a rezoning to expand their cafe into a neighboring Avenues home.** Kara and Tyler Alden want 140 N. B Street and 272 E. 3rd Avenue rezoned from RMF-35 to MU-2. The Planning Commission recommended against it, citing the loss of a single-family home and impacts on neighbors. The council hearing is Oct. 6. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110607.html))
- **Airport would sell about 951 acres of wetlands to the state.** The surplus mitigation land would go to the Utah Department of Natural Resources for a waterfowl management area. Appraisals value the land at $3.8 million to $5.1 million and the easement at up to $571,000. The hearing is Oct. 6. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110603.html))
- **County auditor presents an audit of Discovery Gateway oversight.** The performance audit of the county's oversight of the Discovery Gateway agreement comes Oct. 6. The council also votes that day on 2027 employee benefits, $646,000 for higher Adobe and Microsoft licensing costs and $1.68 million for landfill methane flare design and fencing. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1112491.html))
- **Summum appeals approval of West End Apartments.** The appeal challenges the Planning Commission's design-review decision for the project at about 730 W. 900 S. It argues the approval violates the religious freedom of the neighboring Summum house of worship and the city's design review standards. ([SLC permits & planning](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Planning&TabName=Planning&capID1=26PPA&capID2=00000&capID3=00030&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))
