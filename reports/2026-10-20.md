## Top stories

- **West Valley hotel's pool and spa both closed over safety barriers.** On Oct. 19 county inspectors closed the spa and the pool at Country Inn & Suites, 3422 S Decker Lake Dr. They found spa doors propped open, gates that failed to self-close and latch, high combined chlorine, low pH and a cracked shell. At the pool, they found missing fencing or barriers, doors without self-closing latches and loose handrails. Both remain closed pending repairs; the pool closure is its first suspension in a year. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
- **Cottonwood Heights condo pool shut for second time in a year.** Shadow Ridge Condos' pool, 1244 E Shadow Ridge Dr, was closed Oct. 19 on a follow-up inspection for free chlorine above 10 ppm. As a second permit suspension within a year, it carries a minimum seven-day closure. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
- **Apartment spa closed for excessive chlorine.** Lakeside Village Apartments' spa/pool at 1580 W 3940 S was closed Oct. 19 for free chlorine above 10 ppm, a broken heater thermometer and missing safety signage. It is the first suspension in a year, and the spa has not reopened. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
- **Office building to be razed for "Foundry 43" project.** A demolition permit seeks to remove an existing office building at 537 W 600 S (MU-11 zone) for a project called Foundry 43, at a declared cost of $200,000. ([SLC permits & planning](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Building&TabName=Building&capID1=26BDM&capID2=00000&capID3=00134&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))
