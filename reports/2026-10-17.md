## Top stories

- **Salt Lake County proposes 13.53% property tax hike for new jail.** Mayor Jenny Wilson presents the 2027 budget to the County Council on Oct. 20, with a proposed increase raising $39.5 million for new jail construction, debt service and tax administration. The council will also hold a hearing on up to $375 million in transient room tax revenue bonds and vote on amending the North Temple Viaduct project area agreement with Salt Lake City's CRA and the school district. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1116251.html))
- **Canyons enrollment drops by more than 1,100 as the board weighs a boundary study.** Oct. 6 minutes attached to the Canyons School District Board's Oct. 20 agenda show enrollment falling from 31,499 to 30,365 and MGT Consulting recommended for a boundary-change study. The minutes also note planned housing at The Point and near Jordan Commons and the RSL stadium. The Oct. 20 meeting includes the second reading of the 2027-28 school calendar. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1116379.html))
- **Sunnyside Park slated for $4.2M overhaul.** A Salt Lake City permit for 1735 E Sunnyside Ave covers five lighted pickleball courts, a new playground, a picnic shelter, a perimeter trail, upgraded sports-field lighting, and new irrigation and plantings. ([SLC permits & planning](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Building&TabName=Building&capID1=26HIS&capID2=00000&capID3=08434&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))
- **Health department shuts Via Apartments pool over low chlorine.** The outdoor pool at 3808 S West Temple St was closed Oct. 16 after a follow-up inspection found free chlorine below required levels and gates that don't self-close. It is the pool's first permit suspension within a year, and it has not reopened. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
