## Top stories

- **Murray weighs closing Liberty Elementary.** Murray City School District is considering closing Liberty Elementary starting in 2027-28. The plan would redraw boundaries for Grant, Longview, McMillan and Parkside elementaries and Hillcrest and Riverview middle schools. A public hearing was set for Oct. 1 at the school, and the final board vote is scheduled for Dec. 10. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1108981.html))
- **State school board takes up math standards and AI policy.** The Utah State Board of Education's Oct. 1 agenda listed several action items: a math standards revision following a WestEd review, a model policy on instructional technology and AI, and an AI student task force. The board also took up its 2027 legislative funding and statutory requests and the use of FY2026 carry-forward balances. Other items were the Alcott Learning educator preparation program application, which has an appeal, and school district property tax rate information. The notice does not include outcomes. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1111789.html))
- **Canyons board to discuss hiring a boundary consultant (Tuesday, Oct. 6).** The Canyons School District Board will discuss hiring a boundary and enrollment consultant, which could signal future boundary changes. It will also hold closed sessions on personnel and real property. Attached Sept. 15 minutes show the board set new enrollment minimums for first-grade Dual Language Immersion classes. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1112591.html))
