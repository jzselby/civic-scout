## Top stories

- **Tooele County residents can weigh in on next year's budget Tuesday**: The Tooele County Council holds a public hearing Oct. 27 on Resolution 2026-29, which adopts the tentative 2027 budget. Final adoption is tentatively set for Dec. 1. The same meeting takes up a repeal and replacement of the county procurement ordinance, a new financial policy manual and the auditor's 2026 fraud risk assessment. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1117393.html))
- **Salt Lake City bans private jails and approves new Ballpark-area zoning**: At its Oct. 20 meeting, the City Council prohibited jails and community correctional facilities on private property citywide and approved the Ballpark NEXT zoning regulations. It also set hearings on Expanding Housing Options for Nov. 10 and 17, and the record says the Community Clean Energy Program launches in January 2027. ([SLC Council actions](https://www.slc.gov/council/october-20-2026-meeting-recap/))
- **Forward Party is the biggest backer of Emily Buss's campaign**: Buss's 2026 general report shows $36,545 raised and $32,422 spent. Her two largest donations, $9,500 and $9,250, came from the Forward Party of Utah, and Christy R Walton of Jackson, Wyo., gave $2,500. She paid $25,000 to Eye Contact Media for advertising. ([Campaign finance reports](https://disclosures.utah.gov/Search/AdvancedSearch))

## Connections

- **The airport wetland sale has already gone to public hearing.** Newly posted Sept. 8 work-session minutes record a council briefing on selling 900 acres of airport wetland to the Utah Department of Natural Resources. Two Oct. 6 hearing notices name DNR and Utah Open Lands: one on the [disposition of a significant wetland mitigation parcel](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110603.html) and one on a [conservation easement public benefit analysis](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110605.html). Utah Open Lands also appears on the [Oct. 6 formal agenda](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1112213.html). Together, the records track the deal from briefing to formal action. ([Minutes](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45662))
- **The 1990 S. 500 East townhome project is moving through approvals.** The council approved the townhome rezone 7-0 on Sept. 22, according to the minutes. A preliminary subdivision application for the same address was pending as of Sept. 29 in city permit and planning records. ([Minutes](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45656))

## Also notable

- **SLC Council minutes, Sept. 22:** The council deferred the Northpoint industrial rezone. The ordinance version added a data center prohibition, and the Planning Commission had recommended against the rezone. Speakers, including Rep. Angela Romero, opposed a potential Sinclair/Phillips 66 pipeline. The minutes also record a 0-0 all-abstain vote on a Hispanic Heritage resolution. ([SLC Council actions](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45656))
- **Encore Apartments, 489 E. 400 South:** The health department closed both the pool and the wading pool Oct. 21 for disinfectant levels out of range. Neither has reopened. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
- **Palladio Apartments, 360 S. 200 West:** The wading pool was closed Oct. 21 for free chlorine above 10 ppm and has not reopened. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
