## Top stories

- **Charter board advanced 7 of 12 new school proposals.** Pending Sept. 10 minutes show the State Charter School Board invited full applications from Theodore Roosevelt Military Academy, Great Hearts Utah (6-1 vote), Compass Point, Philip Fox Academy, Peak Mastery (4-3), Keystone Academy of the Arts and Spectrum Academy St. George. It rejected five others and approved a small expansion for Monticello Academy. At its meeting Thursday, Oct. 8, the board will ratify its FY27 spend plan, swear in Adam Gerlach and hold strategic planning. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1113475.html))
- **SLC school board takes up CRA amendments, a city property deal and tax increment policy.** The Salt Lake City School District board's Oct. 6 consent agenda included Salt Lake Community Reinvestment Agency amendments, a proposed property agreement with Salt Lake City and curriculum and instructional materials policy I-7. The board also scheduled discussion of Policy B-3 on tax increment financing and possible closed sessions. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1113449.html))
- **Commercial demolition filed for 100 S West Temple downtown.** A commercial demolition application was filed Oct. 6 for 100 S West Temple St. The record does not describe the structure or the scope of work. (SLC permits & planning; no link in record)
- **King's English Bookshop permits name two co-branded airport stores.** Two permits filed Oct. 6 cover renovations to existing retail stores in the airport terminal (3920 W 900 N). One is 881 sq ft under the project name "Trip Advisor and King's English Bookshop." The other is 605 sq ft under "KSL and King's English Bookshop." Both cover finishes, equipment and some new millwork. (SLC permits & planning; no links in records)
