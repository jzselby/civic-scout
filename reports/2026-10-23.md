## Top stories

- **Provo rezones a manufactured home park for transit-oriented development.** On Oct. 20 the Provo Municipal Council rezoned land at 1095 South 500 West and 1200 South Towne Centre Boulevard from manufactured home park (RM) and regional shopping center (SC3) zones to Interim Transit Oriented Development, with a matching general plan change. That raises questions about whether park residents could be displaced. The council also passed an ordinance barring use of property declared unfit or unsafe. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1117759.html))
- **Demolition permit filed for parts of the Salt Palace and the contemporary art museum downtown.** A $3M permit at 100 S. West Temple would demolish Salt Palace Exhibit Halls 2 and 3, the north meeting-room stack and the Utah Museum of Contemporary Art building, about 224,107 sq ft across two buildings. It is Phase 1 of the county's convention center and entertainment district project. Work is planned for February to September 2027, and the rest of the Salt Palace and Abravanel Hall would stay open. ([SLC permits & planning](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Building&TabName=Building&capID1=26BDM&capID2=00000&capID3=00138&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))
- **Buyer seeks rezoning to replace a Redwood Road church house with rental townhomes.** A prospective buyer has applied for a zoning amendment, which goes to the Planning Commission, to turn a property now used as a church house at 660 N. Redwood Rd. in Salt Lake City into a townhome rental development. ([SLC permits & planning](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SLCREF/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Planning&TabName=Planning&capID1=26HIS&capID2=00000&capID3=08581&agencyCode=SLCREF&IsToShowInspection=))
- **Poplar Grove water-main flood victims could get up to $50,000 in city grants.** Newly posted minutes of the Salt Lake City Council's Sept. 22 work session show a unanimous straw poll to move up to $50,000 from the Tenant and Homeowner Loan Fund into grants for residents displaced or damaged by the Aug. 16 Poplar Grove water main break, regardless of income. The minutes also say the Warm Springs park project is on hold with no definitive dates, and the 600/700 North project faces UDOT-related delays near Redwood Road. ([SLC Council actions](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45679))

## Coming up

- **Nov. 5: Historic Landmark Commission public hearing on a new home and ADU on Capitol Hill.** The hearing covers new construction of a single-family home with an accessory dwelling unit on a vacant lot at about 614 N. Center St. in the Capitol Hill Local Historic District. The request includes modifications for building and wall height, building coverage, setbacks and retaining wall height. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1117505.html))
