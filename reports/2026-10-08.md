## Top stories

- **Davis School District authorizes up to $93M in school bonds**: On Oct. 6 the Davis board authorized up to $93 million in general obligation and refunding bonds for land, new schools and renovations, backed by its taxing power. The district already carries $643.9 million in outstanding GO debt, and estimated principal and interest on the new-money portion is $69.9 million. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1113581.html))
- **Health department shuts Los Panchitos food truck**: The truck at 3232 S 400 E had its permit suspended Oct. 7 after 8 critical violations, including no soap or hand-drying provision and employees preparing food without proper handwashing. It has not reopened. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
- **Murray apartment pool closed by health department**: The Artesian Springs Apartments north pool at 4197 S Main St, Murray, was closed Oct. 7 and has not reopened. The closure reason is not stated. The only inspection report available is from June 23 and lists non-critical issues: missing skimmer weirs and check valves, no operation plan and no soap in a shower. ([SL County health closures](https://public.cdpehs.com/UTEnvPbl/))
