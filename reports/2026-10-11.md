## Top stories

- **Jordan District opens public comment on school boundary changes**: On Tuesday, Oct. 13, the Jordan School District Board takes the first of two required public comments on boundary changes affecting Copper Hills, West Jordan, Riverton, Mountain Ridge, Herriman-area and other schools. It also holds a final vote on a property trade with UDOT, and weighs a $702,345 bid for security window film at 11 secondary schools and revisions to its fee policy. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1114401.html))
- **Summit County fire and mosquito districts plan tax hikes near 30%**: On Wednesday, Oct. 14, the Summit County Council takes up two tax increases. The North Summit Fire District plans one raising about $635,265 (27.62%), and the Summit Mosquito Abatement District one raising about $202,816 (30.77%). The council also receives the manager's 2027 budget and considers Board of Equalization reductions, including a $5 million drop in the value of one secondary residence. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1114451.html))
- **U. trustees vote on campus master plan, Rice-Eccles upgrades and Redwood clinic land**: On Tuesday, Oct. 13, University of Utah trustees act on several items:
  - a Campus Master Plan
  - land acquisition to rebuild the Redwood Health Center
  - premium capital projects at Rice-Eccles Stadium
  - an inpatient build-out in the Kathryn F. Kirk (HCI) building

  Naming items include a dining commons and a baseball stadium concourse. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1114519.html))

## Coming up

- **Tue., Oct. 13: Salt Lake County Council.** The council gets a briefing on the first amendment to the North Temple Viaduct project area interlocal agreement with SLC's Community Reinvestment Agency, the SLC school board and Salt Lake City, and will set a public hearing on it for Oct. 20. It also votes on 2027 ZAP Tier I and Zoo funding and 2026 Tier II recommendations, and hears a 2026-27 revenue update. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1114381.html))
- **Wed., Oct. 14: Point of the Mountain State Land Authority.** The board votes on DDA Amendment #4 from The Point Partners, which covers prepaid ground rent and a profit share for the hotel project. It also votes on a new ground lease with Grant Mackay Demolition Company. A closed session on a project proposal and/or personnel is planned. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1114517.html))
