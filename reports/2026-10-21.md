## Top stories

- **UTA forecast shows reserves going negative by 2030:** A 10-year forecast in the Oct. 14 UTA Transit Commission packet shows the ending balance falling from $310 million in 2026 to –$31.8 million in 2030 and –$777.4 million by 2036. The commission also adopted an internal audit plan. It includes an accounting separation-of-duties audit "selected based on concerns observed by leadership." The commission also launched the UTA Moves 2055 long-range plan and held a closed session on litigation and real property. Commissioner Chris Gamvroulas' written criticism of agenda-setting rules is appended to the August minutes. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1112825.html))
- **House GOP committee gives candidate Jim Alvey $20,000:** Alvey's 2026 General report shows $25,330 raised and $14,267 spent. Most of it came from a $20,000 Oct. 15 contribution from the Utah House Republican Election Committee. Other donors were the Weber County GOP ($2,000) and All in for Utah PAC ($1,000). Top payees were State Inicia ($7,070 combined) and Roost ($4,000). ([Campaign finance reports](https://disclosures.utah.gov/Search/AdvancedSearch))
- **State proposes $3,720 penalty for SR-194 freeway contractor:** The Division of Water Quality offered RLW/CLYDE, a Joint Venture, LP an expedited settlement over alleged construction-dewatering permit violations on the SR-194 Lehi 2100 North Freeway project. Public comment is open through Nov. 6. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1113515.html))
- **$500,000 restaurant conversion planned on State Street:** A permit would remodel a two-story, 3,260 sq ft building at 265 S State St into a restaurant called Cramer House.
