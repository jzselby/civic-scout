## Top stories

- **SLC utility impact fees would roughly triple, the first increase since 1999.** The water impact fee for a 3/4-inch meter would rise from $1,871 to $5,577. The stormwater fee per quarter acre would go from $374 to $1,993, and a new meter-based sewer fee of $4,300 would apply to a 3/4-inch meter. Fees would phase in at 70% on Jan. 1, 2027, and reach full amounts July 1, 2028. The council may vote Oct. 6, along with Budget Amendment No. 1, which includes $11.5M in grants for Fleet Block and 9 Line improvements. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1107993.html); [agenda](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1112213.html))
- **Salt Lake City weighs a citywide ban on new jails and community correctional facilities.** An Oct. 6 hearing covers removing both as conditional uses, which would prohibit them citywide. State- and federally owned property would be exempt. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110613.html))
- **Domo lays off 175 in American Fork.** The Utah tech company filed a WARN notice covering 175 workers, with layoffs dated Sept. 23. ([WARN layoff notices](https://jobs.utah.gov/employer/business/warnnotices.html))
- **Inland Port adds 8,598 acres, tables power campus amid data center criticism.** The board adopted the Black Gold amendment in Roosevelt and the Pony Express amendment. It tabled the Iron Springs amendment, whose Juniper zone is planned for a rail-served industrial and power campus, as public commenters urged a data center moratorium. Staff reported 135 acres of the North Temple Landfill remediated and a plat for 171 developable acres submitted to SLC. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110291.html))
- **Salt Lake County hearing on $128M parks and open space bond.** The county council takes public comment Oct. 6 on up to $128M in general obligation bonds for open space, parks, trails and recreation. Voters decide in a Nov. 3 special election. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1112493.html))
- **Contested Ellerbeck Mansion rezoning goes to council.** Kara and Tyler Alden seek MU-2 zoning at 140 N. B St. and 272 E. 3rd Ave. to expand cafe and retail uses into a neighboring single-family home. The Planning Commission recommended against it, citing loss of housing and impacts on neighbors. The hearing is Oct. 6. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1110607.html))
- **County auditor to present audit of Discovery Gateway oversight.** The performance audit comes Oct. 6. The council also votes on 2027 employee benefits, $646,000 for higher Adobe and Microsoft licensing costs, and $1.68M for landfill methane flare design and fencing. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1112491.html))
- **County cancels $19.8M in Salt Palace projects and reviews jail expansion study.** The Sept. 29 agenda redirects $11M to renovation bond debt service and cancels $3.9M in operating subsidy and $5M in client relief funding. The council also heard a jail expansion feasibility study from Babcock Design Group. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1111217.html))
