## Top stories

- **Inland Port board to vote on $27.6M Iron County tax deal and a possible gas-power site:** On Nov. 5 the Utah Inland Port Authority board votes on a tax-sharing agreement with Diamond S Holdings. The deal would repay up to $27.561M in infrastructure costs at Lakeside Industrial Park from about 88% of the authority's 25-year tax differential. The board also votes on Iron Springs Amendment #5, which adds the pipeline-adjacent Juniper Zone site for possible natural-gas power generation. The agenda further includes new public infrastructure districts in Box Elder and West Weber counties, reconsideration of the Black Gold and Pony Express amendments, and the annual audit. ([Public meeting notices](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/sitemap/notice/1118313.html))
- **HF Sinclair won't rule out running a fuel pipeline through Salt Lake City's watershed and foothills:** Minutes of the council's Oct. 13 work session show every member opposed the Westline Connect refined-fuel pipeline route through the city's watershed and foothills. Company officials said the route follows a 1950s Chevron corridor that has leaked and that most of the county route is a High Consequence Area. They declined to commit to avoiding the city. ([SLC Council actions](https://slc.primegov.com/Portal/Meeting?compiledMeetingDocumentFileId=45727))
